# Sterling Invest paper-v2

`grok-forward-v1` is closed after one real week, 2026-W46. That log is `evidence/forward.jsonl`. It is not rewritten and it is not extended.

`paper-v2` is a separate experiment. Its log is `evidence/invest-v2/forward.jsonl` (mock: `evidence/invest-v2/mock/forward.jsonl`). It starts at £10,000 virtual GBP on its own first real week. The pre-registered window is still at least 104 weekly decisions, 24 months, and 30 completed round trips. Until then the output is a technical pilot, not a performance conclusion.

The operational £2.50 and £5 caps, the empty allowlist, and execution DENY are unchanged. This path does not read those files. `execution_permission` stays `NOT_ISSUED`.

Grok returns target weights for VWRP, CUKX, IGLT, AGBP, SGLN, XSTR, and cash. They must each be between 0 and 1 and sum to 1 within 0.005. Inside that band they are scaled to sum to exactly 1. Outside it, the reply is invalid and the current portfolio is kept. The reply also names `decision` as `ALLOCATE`, `HOLD`, or `NO_TRADE`, plus portfolio-level reasoning, `identified_risks`, and `change_justification`. `HOLD` and `NO_TRADE` keep the current book. A reply that says hold but sends a different book is inconsistent and is not applied. `model_id` in the JSON must match the configured client model. A different name in the body does not change which model was called, and the week fails closed to HOLD.

BUY, SELL, and HOLD on each fund are derived from the gap versus current weight. A gap smaller than 0.005 is left untraded. A fund with no cross-checked price, or a price older than five weekday sessions, is not tradable. Fills use the first XLON session whose 08:00 Europe/London open is strictly after the decision timestamp. A later session is not used, and an earlier open is not used. The live source is the Yahoo daily chart open (`yahoo-open`), converted from GBp to GBP when the feed quotes pence. If that open is missing, the order stays pending and the log says `opening_price_unavailable`. It can still fill on a later published open. If it is still unfilled at the fifth XLON session, the log says `EXPIRED` and that order is not filled afterwards. A newer accepted decision also expires a resting missing-open order. A rejected or HOLD decision does not install the new weights and does not cancel a resting order that was already accepted. A close is not used in place of an open. Yahoo is not an exchange-verified print. A second source, when present for that session, is named on the valuation; otherwise the close is marked as Yahoo only. Spread and slippage still move the fill. Fund OCF is not deducted again: the exchange price is already net of the ongoing charge.

## Genuine Grok research pilot (offline)

Phase 1 prepares an offline research lane. It does not place an order and it does not call the paid API by itself. `execution_permission` stays `NOT_ISSUED`.

Each decision stores the full point-in-time research snapshot, `prompt_version` (`paper-v2-allocation-2026-11-14`), and `prompt_sha256`. The model is sent a compact projection of that snapshot (`paper-v2-prompt-projection-2026-11-14`), with the snapshot's SHA-256, the six latest GBP prices with their quoted currency and GBP divisor, indicator values, the book, SONIA, and the research limits. Raw price history and indicator source-time lists stay in the stored snapshot and are not repeated in the prompt. The risk gate reads the stored snapshot, not the projection.

The deterministic check sits between the parsed reply and `book.pending`. It uses `research-risk-v0`, which stays `owner_approved: false` and is not an operational trading policy. Concentration, turnover, cash, instrument eligibility, stale or missing prices, and next-open timing are rejected in full when they breach. The pilot does not clip or resize a breaching allocation. The previous clipping helper remains available to the older research simulator and is not the pilot gate.

An offline genuine-lane run replays a recorded or synthetic reply. The log path for that lane still ends in `evidence/invest-v2/forward.jsonl`, and the mock lane still ends in `evidence/invest-v2/mock/forward.jsonl`. Tests use a temporary directory. The script refuses the evidence files inside this repository. `scripts/run_invest_v2.py --dry-run --fixture` prints a synthetic request. `--dry-run --cache DIR --as-of TIME` reads local ETF CSVs and, when present, `boe_sonia.csv`. A timezone-aware `published_at` or `provider_published_at` is a verified publication time. `availability_basis=assumed-next-session` makes a close usable at 08:00 Europe/London on the next XLON session, not at the 16:30 close. A file with neither fails closed. Yahoo daily closes have no verified publication time, so they use that next-session assumption. Yahoo GBP closes are stored to 0.0001 GBP, half up, so a JSON float does not keep spurious digits. The cache records `quoted_currency` and `gbp_divisor` beside that normalised price. SONIA still waits until 09:00 Europe/London on the next XLON session. Neither dry-run opens a connection, writes an evidence log, nor places an order. The printed cost is a local pre-send estimate of the full request. It is not a provider invoice cap. `--provider recorded --response-file` replays a local file.

## grok-4.3 request controls

The prepared client is `grok-4.3`, at most one API call per authorised run, with an estimated pre-call ceiling of $0.25. The token ceiling and `max_completion_tokens` stay in force. A response that may already have been billed is not retried. Usage records keep `cost_in_usd_ticks` when the provider reports a cost.

Official rates checked on 13 Nov 2026, from the grok-4.3 model page and the xAI pricing page: short context under 200,000 tokens is $1.25 / 1M input, $0.20 / 1M cached input, and $2.50 / 1M output. At or above 200,000 tokens those rates are $2.50, $0.40, and $5.00. A US regional endpoint is 1.1 times those rates; the `api.x.ai` estimate does not apply that multiple. The pre-call figure assumes uncached input and the output budget. Reasoning tokens and the invoice can be higher. No paid call is part of this change.

## Benchmarks and comparison

Valid benchmark rows are marked `benchmark_calculation: point-in-time-v1` and `benchmark_status: valid`. Frozen trend and dual-momentum rules decide at each month-end from closes on or before that session, then trade at a later session's close. Static arms enter at the first session close. This series has no opens, so a close is not used in place of a missing open, and `execution_equivalence` is `not_claimed`. The arms are provisional and are not fully execution-equivalent to each other or to the paper book's next open. SONIA for a historical day is the print published by 16:30 London that day, and not a print that appears only because the report clock is later. A close with a `published_at` after that session's clock is excluded.

A stored performance row without `point-in-time-v1` is a legacy result. It is left as it was written. It needs recalculation before it is compared with a valid row. The evidence files are not rewritten to replace it.

Cash earns published SONIA, Bank of England series IUDSOIA, minus 0.50 percentage points, and never less than zero. The Bank publishes each print at 09:00 Europe/London on the next XLON business day. A valuation uses a print only after that time, so today's SONIA is not used today. If no print is available yet, that day accrues nothing and the valuation says `sonia_not_yet_published`. A later session posts the interest once, when the print exists. The same series and the same lag are used for the live book. Historical benchmark days use the print that had been published by that day's own 16:30 London clock.

The daily job is `daily-invest.yml`. It is manual only, forced off with `if: false`, and it does not call a model.

Sterling FX is not built. The record carries `system` (`sterling-invest`, later `sterling-fx`) and `experiment`. A later FX book would use the same performance JSON schema, `sterling-performance-v1`, and its own evidence lane.
